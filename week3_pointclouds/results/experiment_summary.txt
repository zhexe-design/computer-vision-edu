HOW TO CHOOSE THE BEST RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------
Compare the saved images and point clouds.
Prefer a result that removes isolated noise while
preserving the object's shape and fine details.
The largest percentage of removed points does not
necessarily indicate the best result.
